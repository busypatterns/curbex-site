Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026  ·  CurbexUSA, Fort Worth, TX

CurbexUSA ("we," "us," or "our") provides sign rental services in the Dallas–Fort Worth metropolitan area. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including name, phone, email, business name, and service address submitted through our quote form, payment information (handled securely via our payment processor), and communications you send us.

We also automatically collect: IP address and general location, browser and device type, pages visited and time on page, and cookies and similar tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to quote requests, process and manage your rental agreement, schedule installation and removal, send service communications and invoices, send marketing communications (opt out anytime), improve our website, and comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share it with service providers bound by confidentiality, local DFW permitting authorities where required, law enforcement when required by law, or a successor entity in a merger or acquisition.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described here, including rental records, and as required by law. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our site may use essential cookies and analytics cookies (e.g. Google Analytics). You can control cookies through your browser. We do not use cookies to track you across third-party websites.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before submitting personal information.

7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. Contact us immediately if you believe we have done so.

9. Your Rights & Choices

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of marketing at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Texas Privacy Notice (TDPSA)

Texas residents have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), including the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of personal data we process, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal data or targeted advertising. We will respond within 45 days of a verifiable request.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
